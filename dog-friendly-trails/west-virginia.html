Before you set off with a Springer

  • Read the sign at the trailhead: leash rules and seasonal closures change faster than the map.
  • Off-leash means a recall you trust around wildlife and other dogs. The adolescence training reset helps if yours has gone deaf to it.
  • Plan the walk as part of the day. The exercise routines guide shows how owners balance runs, sniff walks and training.
  • Wet trail, muddy dog: set the car up first with the muddy car setup.
