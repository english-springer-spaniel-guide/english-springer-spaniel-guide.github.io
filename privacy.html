Privacy Policy

English Springer Spaniel Guide is a static GitHub Pages site with no sign-ups, no forms and no newsletter, so we collect no personal information.

Cookies aren't used. A reading counter, and any values typed into a tool, remain in localStorage on your device. GitHub may keep standard logs, IP addresses included, under its privacy statement.

Videos load via youtube-nocookie.com. Linked websites apply their own policies. Get in touch through contact.
