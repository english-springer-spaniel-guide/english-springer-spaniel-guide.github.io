05How the directory works

From the map to your walk

  1. Mapped by volunteers

    OpenStreetMap contributors tag paths with a dog rule: allowed, on leash or off leash.

  2. Filtered

    We keep named public paths and hiking routes, and drop sidewalks, private paths and businesses.

  3. Mapped by state

    Each state gets a map with every trail, its length, surface and the nearest town.

  4. Checked on site

    You read the sign at the trailhead: local rules change, and the sign always wins.

English Springer Spaniel Guide is written with AI assistance and checked by a person. We list public trails only: no businesses, no sponsored places. Read the editorial policy.
